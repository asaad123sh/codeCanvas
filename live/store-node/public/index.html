Frequently asked

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery is 3-5 business days, express is 1-2. Store pickup is ready in about 4 hours.

Can I pay the courier in cash?

Yes — cash on delivery is available on every order. You choose it at checkout and tell the courier how much to collect.

What if I am not home?

The courier will try twice. You can allow rescheduling at checkout, or leave a note with a safe place.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to 20 countries. Duties are calculated at checkout before you confirm the order.
